Anvil

A workout log for iPhone. Privacy and support.

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Privacy Policy

Anvil collects no data. There is no account, no sign in, and no server for the app to talk to.

Where your training data lives

Exercises, sessions, sets, routines and settings are stored only in a database on your device. They never leave it.

What Anvil does not do

  • No network connection — the app contacts no servers.
  • No analytics, no usage statistics, no crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No sharing of data with third parties.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location or health data.

Backup files

Anvil can export your data to a file. That file is created only when you start the export yourself, and where it goes is entirely your choice through the iOS share sheet. The app does not upload it anywhere.

Your iPhone's iCloud backup

If you use Apple's device backup, it may include Anvil's data — as it does for any other app. That is a feature of iOS and is governed by Apple's privacy policy; Anvil itself transmits nothing.

Deleting your data

Deleting Anvil from your device removes all of its data. Nothing is left behind, because nothing is stored anywhere else.

Changes

If any of this ever changes, it will be stated here, with a new date, before a version ships that it applies to.

Support

Questions, bugs and requests belong on the project page: github.com/timonheitz/anvil/issues. A bug report is most useful with your device, iOS version, and what you did just before it happened.
